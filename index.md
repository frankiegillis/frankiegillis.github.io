In the above graph, the vertices labelled $1$, $2$, $3$, and $4$ have pairwise-isomorphic vertex-deleted subgraphs, but no automorphism of the graph maps one onto the other. Vertices with this property are called *pseudo-similar*. Graphs containing pseudo-similar vertices were the focus of my 2025 summer research.
